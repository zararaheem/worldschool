Build 2

The Cohort Experience

Testing
Anticipating breaking points, design instinct, cultural humility, cohort resilience
Time
1.5–2 hours
Deliverable
Two links: experience design + 3-min walkthrough video

Design something that prevents a cohort from breaking. Pick one design constraint:

  • Assume your cohort has conflict by week 5.
  • Assume energy drops by mid-rotation.
  • Assume cultural missteps happen.
  • Assume someone wants to go home by week 10.

See the Worked Examples tab for what strong submissions look like.

Build 3

The Video

Testing
Self-awareness, honesty, mindset
Time
20 minutes
Deliverable
One 90-second to 2-minute video

Talk to us. 90 seconds to 2 minutes. Phone-quality is fine. Don't script. Don't read. Two questions: (1) What are you most excited about for this year? (2) What do you understand your role to be on this trip? Be specific — not what you hope it will be, what you actually believe it is.

Worked Examples

Strong submissions for Build 2 — The Cohort Experience. Click any constraint to see what good looks like.

What Makes a Submission Strong vs. Weak
Strong
  • A repeating structural feature, not a one-time event
  • Specifies sequence, timing, prompts, what the guide says, what students do
  • Names what happens when it goes sideways
  • Cultural humility built in — local community as co-facilitators, not scenery
Weak
  • Vague principle (e.g., "we'll have honest conversations")
  • A one-time workshop or off-the-shelf program that doesn't fit the cohort
  • Local community featured but not consulted
  • Design treats the local community as a teaching prop
